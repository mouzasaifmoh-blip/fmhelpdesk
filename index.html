How it works
Simple for teachers, powerful for supervisors
Teachers scan a QR code or visit this site, fill in a quick form, and the FM supervisor is notified instantly.
📋
Bilingual Submission
Teachers submit in English or Arabic. Categories include Maintenance (AC, Lights, Leakage, Electrical) and Cleaning (Spill, Odor, Pest Control).
📷
Photo Attachment
Teachers can attach up to 3 photos of the issue directly from their phone camera or gallery for faster diagnosis.
🔔
Instant Notifications
The FM supervisor receives an email alert the moment a ticket is submitted, with priority flagged in the subject line.
📊
Live Dashboard
Track all open, in-progress, and resolved tickets in one place. Filter by category, priority, or location.
📄
Monthly Reports
Auto-generated monthly summary sent to the supervisor on the 1st of every month — no manual work needed.
📱
QR Code Access
Post QR codes in classrooms and corridors. Teachers scan and land directly on the submission form — no app needed.
For Teachers · للمعلمين
Scan to report an issue
Post this QR code in every classroom and corridor. Teachers scan with any phone camera — no app, no login needed.
